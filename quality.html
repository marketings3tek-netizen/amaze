Why We Obsess Over This

We build the way we would for our own family.

A structural engineer signs off on a pour once. After that, the concrete is sealed inside a column or a slab for the life of the building — so the only chance to get it right is before it's poured, not after. We treat every batch that way, whether it's a boundary wall or a hospital.

That's why we don't test concrete once, at the end. We test the raw material before it's ours, the mix before it leaves the plant, and the batch again on your site — care, checked three times over.

Three Checkpoints

Tested at the source, in the lab, and on your site.

01

At the Quarry

Aggregate is sourced from our own crusher units, so quality control starts before the material is even ours to sell — not after a supplier hands it over.

02

In the Lab

Every ingredient is tested in-house to QCI standards and cross-verified by NABL accredited labs as per IS:4926 — cement, aggregates, water, and admixtures, on a documented schedule.

03

On Your Site

Slump and temperature are checked on every single truck. Our QC Engineers and site coordinators stay through the pour — not a phone call away, present.

People, Not Just Paperwork

Who's actually watching the batch.

  • Production officers with a minimum of 15 years' experience overseeing every mix.
  • Statistical process control on every batch, to hit designed strength consistently, not just on average.
  • A newly automated Schwing Stetter batching plant with E-Biller software — 100% automatic batching and delivery invoicing, so there's a digital record of what actually went into your pour.
  • Site coordinators and QC Engineers present for every delivery, watching the one part of the process you can't undo.
