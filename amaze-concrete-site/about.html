Since 1994

Thirty years, from raw material to ready-mix.

Amaze Concrete Products Pvt. Ltd. began as a raw-material supplier — sourcing and crushing its own aggregate for Chennai's builders — before moving directly into ready-mix concrete. That means we control quality at the source, not just at the batching plant, which is where almost every other RMC supplier in the city starts.

Today we run a modern, automated batching plant, an in-house NABL-aligned testing lab, and a fleet of transit mixers and pumps — serving builders, contractors, engineers, and developers across Chennai with over 1000 projects delivered.

  • Own crusher units & aggregate quarry — quality controlled from source
  • Schwing Stetter automated batching plant with E-Biller software
  • In-house lab to QCI standards, cross-verified by NABL accredited labs
  • Site coordinators & QC Engineers present at every pour
